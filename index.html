Privacy Policy for Rescue Grid

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

1. Overview

Rescue Grid ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as an offline-first puzzle game. We value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information when you play Rescue Grid.

2. Information Collection and Use

No Personal Information Collected: Rescue Grid does not require account creation, registration, or login. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

Local Device Storage: The App stores your game progress (unlocked missions, stars earned, move records, audio settings, and daily challenge streak) locally on your device using standard local preferences. This data remains on your device and is not shared or uploaded to any remote server.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

In this release, Rescue Grid contains no third-party advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking services. The game functions completely offline.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our game does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. Rescue Grid contains family-friendly, non-violent puzzle gameplay.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically if new features (such as cloud saves) are introduced in future releases. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
